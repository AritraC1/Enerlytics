Limits of the data

The model was trained on 1,000 curated records, of which 907 carried a PESTLE label — a small dataset by machine-learning standards. The classes are far from balanced: Economic and Industries dominate, while Social, Organization, Lifestyles and Technological have only a handful of examples, so their scores are fragile and the confusion matrix confirms the model mostly confuses them with the big classes. Confidences shown on the Predict page are softmax-normalized decision scores from a LinearSVC — relative rankings, not calibrated probabilities. Finally, the data is a point-in-time snapshot of energy news titles, so performance on fresh, differently-worded text is untested.
